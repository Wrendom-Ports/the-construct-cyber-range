### 🕹️ The Logic: Narrative JSON Orchestration
Unlike traditional static labs, **The Construct** utilizes a proprietary JSON-driven mission delivery system. 

* **Missions-as-Code:** Curriculum is injected into the workspace via dynamic payloads, allowing for real-time scenario evolution.
* **TTC Topology Mapping:** A high-fidelity network map based on the **Toronto Subway System**, providing a familiar yet complex architectural landscape for defensive maneuvers.
* **The Ghost Layer:** Every mission or scenario runs on a decoupled, multi-geo node infrastructure. When the student completes a mission, the environment undergoes a **Zero-State Purge**.

### 🚀 Public Entry Missions: The Urban Defense Series
*Simplified scenarios designed for new recruits and the general public to understand the power of Ghost Ports.*

#### **[MISSION: THE COFFEE-SHOP CLONE]**
* **Scenario:** You are working remotely at a cafe near **Eglinton Station**. A hacker has set up a fake Wi-Fi hotspot to steal your login credentials.
* **Objective:** Detect the "Evil Twin" signal and activate the **Ghost Pipe**.
* **Outcome:** Your traffic is instantly encrypted and tunneled to a secure Shield VPS. The hacker sees nothing but scrambled code.

#### **[MISSION: THE PHANTOM PHONE-CALL]**
* **Scenario:** A suspicious "IT Admin" is calling, claiming your account at **Queen’s Park** has been breached. They want your IP address to "verify" your identity.
* **Objective:** Do not reveal your location. Use the **Shield Rotation** tool.
* **Outcome:** You give them a temporary Shield IP. By the time they try to scan it, you’ve collapsed the node and vanished. The "door" they were aiming for is gone.

#### **[MISSION: THE SUBWAY SNIFFER]**
* **Scenario:** You're commuting on the **Yellow Line**. Someone on the same train is using a "sniffer" tool to see what apps people are using.
* **Objective:** Enable **Invisibility Mode** (Decoupled Egress).
* **Outcome:** The sniffer tool shows your seat as empty. Your data exists in the "Ghost Layer" while you travel physically through the station.

#### **[MISSION: THE REVENGE OF THE GLITCH]**
* **Scenario:** A basic computer virus is trying to lock your files (Ransomware) while you're at **Dundas Square**. 
* **Objective:** Trigger a **Zero-State Purge**.
* **Outcome:** Instead of paying the hacker, you delete the entire environment and restart from your last clean "Soul" backup in under one second. The virus is deleted; your files remain safe.
