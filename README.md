### 🚀 Mission Tier 1: The Urban Defense Series
*Simplified scenarios designed to build essential "Digital Street Smarts" for the everyday user.*

#### **[MISSION: THE CAFE CLONE]**
* **Real-World Threat:** You're at a cafe near **Eglinton Station**. You see two networks: "Cafe_Guest" and "Cafe_Guest_FREE." One is real, the other is a trap set by a hacker to intercept your banking password. You accidentally connect to the fake Wi-Fi
* **The Skill:** **Tunneling & Verification.** Trainees learn to never trust "Open" Wi-Fi. 
* **Objective:** Activate your encrypted tunnel (Ghost Pipe) and verify the "Lock" icon in your browser before entering sensitive data.
* **Skill Learned - Operational Security:** Even though you accidentally connected to the "Clone" Wi-Fi network, because you're using a VPN(enrypted tunnel,the hacker only sees scrambled noise, not your password.

#### **[MISSION: THE ACCIDENTAL CLICK]**
* **Real-World Threat:** While at **Dundas Square**, you get a "Canada Post" text about a missed delivery. You click the link before you realize it looks fake.
* **The Skill:** **Sandboxing & Session Isolation.** Learning to open "risky" links in a separate, temporary environment.
* **Objective:** Send the link to a **Sovereign VDI** (Throwaway Browser) to inspect the threat safely.
* **Skill Learned - Suspicious Link Identification and Management:** The malicious site tries to download a file, but because it's in an isolated RAM-only session, it can't touch your actual phone. You close the tab, and the threat is purged.

#### **[MISSION: THE INBOX INSPECTOR]**
* **Real-World Threat:** You’re at **Union Station** checking your work email. A message arrives from "HR" regarding an urgent "Payroll Update."
* **The Skill:** **Metadata Analysis.** Learning to spot "Spoofing" by looking at the technical source, not the pretty logo.
* **Objective:** Use the **Scanner** to reveal the "Return-Path" and identify the mismatch between the display name and the actual source domain.
* **Skill Learned - Phishing Email Identiication:** Successfully flagging the email as "Phishing" prevents simulated credential theft.
